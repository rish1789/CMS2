# 038 — Unified Real-Time Inbox

**Module:** Discovery & Notifications
**Status:** Ready for spec-kit intake

## User Story
As front-desk staff (ClinicAdmin or Operations), I want a single, real-time, claim-based inbox of outstanding work items across the clinic, so that my team can coordinate who is handling what without duplicated effort or dropped tasks.

## Context
The Inbox is a cross-cutting, staff-facing aggregation surface — it doesn't originate its own business events so much as surface work items generated by several other features (walk-in queue entries, waitlist offers, de-verification review flags, etc.) in one real-time, claimable list. It is distinct from the patient-facing Notification pipeline (036/037). BDD §2, §5 (Clinic 1─N Inbox Item).

## Business Rules
- The Inbox is **real-time** — new items appear for all relevant staff without a manual refresh (e.g. via WebSocket/SSE, consistent with the doc's mention of a REST/WebSocket/SSE surface at the technical level, though the exact transport is a technical decision for the plan phase).
- The Inbox is **claim-based**: an item can be claimed by exactly one staff member at a time, so two front-desk staff don't duplicate work (e.g. both calling the same patient). Once claimed, the item should visibly show who claimed it to other staff viewing the same Inbox.
- The Inbox is scoped per clinic — `Clinic 1─N Inbox Item` — staff only see items for their own clinic.
- Item types the Inbox surfaces include (non-exhaustive, drawn from other features in this backlog):
  - Walk-in patients awaiting insertion into a session (feeds from 020).
  - Waitlist offers awaiting patient claim/decline within their 30-minute window, for staff visibility even though the patient can self-claim (feeds from 029).
  - De-verification review/cascade notices — e.g. "Dr. X's license was revoked, N future bookings were auto-cancelled" (feeds from 008).
  - Any other clinic-scoped, actionable work item introduced by future features that needs staff coordination.
- Claiming an item does not necessarily resolve it — claiming signals ownership; resolving/completing it is a separate action once the underlying task is done (e.g. the walk-in has been seated).
- An unclaimed item remains visible to all staff at that clinic; a claimed item is visibly attributed but should still typically remain visible (not hidden) so the team retains situational awareness.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given two staff members are viewing the Inbox for the same clinic, when a new walk-in patient work item is created, then it appears in real time in both staff members' views without a manual refresh.
- Given an unclaimed Inbox item, when one staff member claims it, then the item is marked as claimed by that staff member, and this claim status is reflected in real time to the other staff member viewing the same Inbox.
- Given an item is already claimed by Staff A, when Staff B attempts to claim the same item, then the system rejects the second claim (or otherwise prevents simultaneous dual-ownership).
- Given a de-verification cascade auto-cancels several bookings for a doctor (008), when that cascade completes, then a corresponding Inbox item is created at the affected clinic(s) summarizing the impact.
- Given a waitlist offer is made to a patient (029), when the offer is created, then a corresponding Inbox item appears for staff, even though the patient also has a self-service claim path.
- Given a staff member at Clinic A, when they view their Inbox, then they see only items scoped to Clinic A, never items from other clinics.

## Dependencies
- Depends on: 020-walk-in-priority-insertion — walk-in queue items feed the Inbox.
- Depends on: 029-self-service-waitlist-claim — waitlist offer items feed the Inbox.
- Depends on: 008-deverification-cascade-auto-cancel-bookings — cascade notices feed the Inbox.
- Related: 036-notification-event-pipeline-opt-in-out — a conceptually separate, patient-facing pipeline; do not conflate the two even though some underlying triggers overlap.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Patient-facing visibility into the Inbox — this is a staff-only surface.
- Cross-clinic visibility for any role below Super Admin — Inbox items are strictly clinic-scoped.
- Automatic item resolution/expiry logic beyond what's explicitly defined by the source feature that created the item (e.g. a waitlist offer item's own 30-minute window governs its own lifecycle, per 029).
