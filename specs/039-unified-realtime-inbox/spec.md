# Feature Specification: Unified Real-Time Inbox

**Feature Branch**: `039-unified-realtime-inbox`

**Created**: 2026-09-05

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "038 — Unified Real-Time Inbox. As front-desk staff (ClinicAdmin or Operations), I want a single, real-time, claim-based inbox of outstanding work items across the clinic, so that my team can coordinate who is handling what without duplicated effort or dropped tasks. The Inbox is a cross-cutting, staff-facing aggregation surface that surfaces work items generated by other features (walk-in queue entries, waitlist offers, de-verification review flags) in one real-time, claimable list, distinct from the patient-facing Notification pipeline (036/037)."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-05

- Q: When a patient referenced by an Inbox Item is later anonymized (033) or purged (034), should the Inbox Item's patient-identifying content also be scrubbed? → A: Scrub on anonymize — Inbox Items render patient-identifying summary content live from the referenced Patient/Booking/Waitlist Entry rather than storing a frozen copy, so 033's existing name/phone scrubbing automatically propagates with no new purge logic needed.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - View a real-time, clinic-scoped work item feed (Priority: P1)

As front-desk staff (ClinicAdmin or Operations) at a clinic, I want to see all outstanding work items for my clinic in one list that updates live, so that I always have an accurate, current picture of what needs attention without refreshing the page or asking a colleague.

**Why this priority**: Without a shared, live view, this feature delivers no coordination value at all — it is the foundation every other capability (claiming, resolving) sits on top of. It is independently valuable even before claiming exists: simply seeing all outstanding work in one place is already an improvement over the status quo (nothing).

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by opening the Inbox for a clinic, triggering a walk-in insertion, a waitlist offer, and a de-verification cascade in turn, and confirming each produces a corresponding item that appears in the list without a manual refresh — and that a second clinic's Inbox never shows any of them.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** two staff members are viewing the Inbox for the same clinic, **when** a new work item is created for that clinic, **then** it appears in both staff members' views in real time without a manual refresh.
2. **Given** a staff member at Clinic A, **when** they view their Inbox, **then** they see only items scoped to Clinic A, never items from other clinics.
3. **Given** a de-verification cascade (008) auto-cancels bookings for a doctor at a clinic, **when** the cascade completes, **then** a corresponding Inbox item appears at each affected clinic summarizing the doctor and the number of bookings cancelled.
4. **Given** a waitlist offer (029) is made to a patient, **when** the offer is created, **then** a corresponding Inbox item appears for staff at the relevant clinic, even though the patient also has a self-service claim path.
5. **Given** a walk-in patient is inserted into a session (020), **when** the insertion completes, **then** a corresponding Inbox item appears at that clinic identifying the walk-in and the slot they were placed into.

---

### User Story 2 - Claim an item to establish ownership (Priority: P1)

As front-desk staff, I want to claim an outstanding item so that my colleagues know I'm already handling it, and I want the system to stop a colleague from claiming the same item after I have, so that we never duplicate work (e.g. both calling the same patient).

**Why this priority**: Claiming is the feature's core stated value ("claim-based") and the specific problem this feature exists to solve (duplicated effort). Without it, the Inbox is just a read-only activity log with no coordination benefit — tied for P1 with visibility itself, since one without the other has little value.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by having two staff sessions view the same unclaimed item, having one claim it, and confirming the other sees it become claimed in real time and cannot also claim it.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an unclaimed Inbox item, **when** one staff member claims it, **then** the item is marked as claimed by that staff member, and this status is reflected in real time to every other staff member viewing the same Inbox.
2. **Given** an item already claimed by Staff A, **when** Staff B attempts to claim the same item, **then** the system rejects Staff B's claim and the item remains attributed to Staff A only.
3. **Given** an item is claimed by Staff A, **when** any staff member at that clinic views the Inbox, **then** the item remains visible (not hidden) and clearly shows it is claimed by Staff A.

---

### User Story 3 - Resolve a claimed item once the work is done (Priority: P2)

As the staff member who claimed an item, I want to mark it resolved once I've actually finished the underlying task (e.g. the walk-in has been seated), so that the Inbox reflects only genuinely outstanding work.

**Why this priority**: This closes the loop on an item's lifecycle. It's rated below claiming/visibility because a team can still coordinate effectively (if imperfectly, with a growing list) using just P1 — resolution is what keeps the list clean and trustworthy over a full working day, but the feature already delivers its core duplicated-effort prevention without it.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by claiming an item, marking it resolved, and confirming it no longer appears as an outstanding item to any staff member at that clinic while remaining claimed-and-resolved in any historical view.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an item claimed by Staff A, **when** Staff A marks it resolved, **then** it is removed from the outstanding-items view for every staff member at that clinic in real time.
2. **Given** an item claimed by Staff A, **when** Staff B (who did not claim it) attempts to mark it resolved, **then** the system rejects the attempt.
3. **Given** a waitlist-offer Inbox item, **when** the underlying waitlist offer is claimed, declined, or expires (per 029's own lifecycle), **then** the corresponding Inbox item is automatically resolved without requiring a staff member to claim or resolve it manually.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens if the staff member who claimed an item logs out or their session ends without resolving it? The item remains claimed by them (visible to the team) until either they resolve it later or another mechanism (not part of this feature) releases it — no automatic timeout is introduced.
- What happens if two staff members attempt to claim the same unclaimed item at the exact same moment? Exactly one claim succeeds; the other is rejected as if the item were already claimed.
- What happens if a doctor's de-verification cascade affects bookings across multiple clinics? A separate Inbox item is created at each affected clinic, each scoped to that clinic's own affected bookings only.
- What happens to an item's visibility if the staff member who claimed it is later deactivated or reassigned? The item remains visibly attributed to that staff member's identity; this feature does not reassign or re-open items on staff deactivation.
- What happens when a staff member wants to hand off an item they claimed to a colleague, without it being finished? The claimant can release their own claim, returning the item to unclaimed so anyone (including themselves again) can claim it.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST maintain a list of Inbox Items, each scoped to exactly one clinic.
- **FR-002**: System MUST create an Inbox Item, scoped to the affected clinic, when a walk-in patient is inserted into a session (020).
- **FR-003**: System MUST create an Inbox Item, scoped to the relevant clinic, when a waitlist offer is made to a patient (029).
- **FR-004**: System MUST create an Inbox Item, scoped to each affected clinic, summarizing the doctor and the count of auto-cancelled bookings, when a de-verification cascade (008) completes.
- **FR-005**: System MUST push newly created and updated Inbox Items to all staff currently viewing that clinic's Inbox without requiring a manual refresh.
- **FR-006**: System MUST show staff only Inbox Items scoped to a clinic they are actively staffed at, and MUST NOT show items from any other clinic.
- **FR-007**: System MUST allow a staff member (ClinicAdmin or Operations) to claim any currently unclaimed Inbox Item at a clinic they are staffed at.
- **FR-008**: System MUST prevent an Inbox Item from being claimed by more than one staff member at a time; a claim attempt on an already-claimed item MUST be rejected.
- **FR-009**: System MUST make a claimed item's claimant identity visible to every staff member viewing that clinic's Inbox, and MUST keep a claimed item visible (not hidden) to the whole team.
- **FR-010**: System MUST allow the staff member who currently holds a claim on an item to release that claim, returning the item to unclaimed.
- **FR-011**: System MUST allow only the staff member who currently holds the claim on an item to mark it resolved.
- **FR-012**: System MUST remove a resolved item from the outstanding-items view shown to staff, while retaining its record.
- **FR-013**: System MUST automatically resolve a waitlist-offer Inbox Item when its underlying waitlist offer is claimed, declined, or expires, independent of whether any staff member claimed it.
- **FR-014**: System MUST NOT expose any Inbox Item or Inbox capability to patients.
- **FR-015**: System MUST NOT introduce any automatic resolution or expiry timing for an Inbox Item beyond what FR-013 defines for waitlist-offer items; all other item types resolve only via explicit staff action (FR-011).
- **FR-016**: System MUST derive any patient-identifying content shown in an Inbox Item's summary from the current state of the referenced Patient/Booking/Waitlist Entry record at display time, rather than storing a frozen, independent copy of that content — so that a later anonymization (033) or retention purge (034) of the referenced patient is automatically reflected in every Inbox Item that mentions them, with no separate scrubbing step.

### Key Entities

- **Inbox Item**: A single outstanding work item surfaced to staff at one clinic. Key attributes: the clinic it belongs to, its type (walk-in, waitlist offer, de-verification cascade notice, or a future type), a reference to the entity it summarizes (not a frozen summary string — see FR-016), its current state (unclaimed / claimed / resolved), which staff member (if any) currently claims it, and when it was created. Related to exactly one Clinic (Clinic 1─N Inbox Item, per the source design). Not related to any single other domain entity uniformly — the item type determines which entity it references (a Slot/Booking for walk-ins, a Waitlist Entry for offers, a Doctor Profile plus affected-booking count for cascade notices).

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A newly created work item appears in every relevant staff member's Inbox view within 5 seconds, with no manual refresh required.
- **SC-002**: Two staff members can never both believe they own the same work item — 100% of concurrent claim attempts on the same item result in exactly one successful claim and one rejection.
- **SC-003**: Staff at one clinic never see a work item belonging to a different clinic, verified across all item types.
- **SC-004**: A staff member can determine, at a glance, which outstanding items are unclaimed and which are already being handled (and by whom) without navigating away from the Inbox view.
- **SC-005**: Resolved items no longer clutter the outstanding view, keeping the list staff see limited to items that still need attention.

## Assumptions

- **Walk-in item timing**: The walk-in Inbox item is created once the walk-in insertion (020) has actually completed, rather than representing a pre-insertion "arrival" queue — no feature in this backlog defines a walk-in arrival/registration concept separate from the insertion action itself, and inventing one would exceed this feature's own scope (Constitution Principle II, Simplicity & YAGNI). The item exists for team coordination and audit visibility around who handled the walk-in, not as a gate staff must pass through before performing the insertion.
- **Manual resolution default**: Item types with no defined auto-resolution lifecycle of their own (walk-in insertions, de-verification cascade notices) are resolved only by an explicit staff action from the current claimant (FR-011), since no source feature defines any other closure signal for them.
- **Claimant-only resolution**: Only the current claimant may resolve an item, consistent with the claim itself signaling accountable ownership; any other staff member wanting to resolve it must first claim it (after the current claimant releases their own claim, if held).
- **Ordering**: Items are presented oldest-unclaimed-first by default, matching a front-desk team's natural first-in-first-handled work distribution; no other ordering/prioritization rule is defined.
- **Staff scope**: "Staff" for claim/resolve/view purposes means ClinicAdmin or Operations role, actively staffed at the item's clinic — the same staffing/authorization concept already used by 020/025/026/027's own staff-facing actions.
- **Transport**: The real-time delivery mechanism (WebSocket, SSE, or another push technology) is a technical decision left to the planning phase; this specification only requires that updates reach viewing staff without a manual refresh.
- **No new domain event sources**: This feature only consumes signals already produced by 020, 029, and 008's existing, already-converged code paths — it introduces no new business rule into those features and does not change their own behavior.
